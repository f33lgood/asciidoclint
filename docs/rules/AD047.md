# AD047 - abstract-placement

Tags: core, structure
Severity: warning
Fixability: no.
Fix helper: Use `[abstract]` on a level-1 article section before normal body
sections, or remove it.

What it is used for: An abstract is a short summary of an article. It states
the purpose and scope of the document before the main body begins.

Necessity: Asciidoctor documents `abstract` as an article special section.
Asciidoctor.js accepts misplaced and nested abstract markers silently, and in
book documents normalizes them to ordinary chapters. AD047 restricts source to
the documented article placement instead of accepting parser-normalized
structure.

Bad:

```adoc
= Article

== Body

[abstract]
=== Nested Abstract
```

Good:

```adoc
= Article

[abstract]
== Abstract

Summary.

== Body
```

AsciiDoc/Asciidoctor basis: The documented abstract is article front matter
before normal sections. In `asciidoctor.js`, `[abstract]` on nested sections is
still exposed as an abstract section without a useful diagnostic, and in books
the section is parsed as a chapter. AD047 reports the mismatch so authors do
not rely on permissive parser behavior.
