# AD049 - glossary-placement

Tags: core, structure
Severity: warning
Fixability: no.
Fix helper: Use `[glossary]` before a level-1 glossary section, or a level-0
glossary only when it is adjacent to book parts.

What it is used for: A glossary defines terms, acronyms, and domain-specific
phrases used in the document. It is commonly placed near the front or back of
technical documentation.

Necessity: Asciidoctor documents `glossary` as a special section style for
articles and books. Asciidoctor.js accepts nested or block-applied glossary
markers silently. AD049 restricts glossary markers to documented section levels
so a glossary is visible as document structure.

Bad:

```adoc
= Article

== Body

[glossary]
=== Terms
```

Good:

```adoc
= Article

[glossary]
== Glossary

term:: Definition.
```

AsciiDoc/Asciidoctor basis: Article glossaries are level-1 sections. Book
glossaries may be level 1 or level 0 when they are adjacent to parts.
Asciidoctor.js exposes nested glossary sections and block styles without a
diagnostic, so AD049 reports source that does not match the documented levels.
